NOVA is a private social application for communicating and sharing with people you choose. Users are responsible for the content they send and for following applicable law.

NOVA may use Google for authentication and project-operated Cloudflare infrastructure for account bootstrap, device registration, Space admission, rendezvous, and encrypted control transport. Those third-party services remain subject to their own terms.

NOVA is provided on a best-effort basis while the private beta is under active development. Network availability is not guaranteed. Peer-to-peer features may expose participants' network addresses to other participants.

Do not use NOVA to distribute unlawful content, attack systems, impersonate another person, or bypass another person's access controls.

These private-beta terms should receive legal review before a broad public launch.
